= Application Platform Litmus Test
:navtitle: Application Platform Litmus Test

In this lesson, you'll learn a simple way to evaluate whether a solution qualifies as a modern *Application Platform*.

TIP: As you progress through this lesson, think about your own organization. Does your current platform provide these capabilities as an integrated experience or through separate products?

== The Four Essential Capabilities

Every modern *Application Platform* should provide the following foundational capabilities.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Capability | Why It Matters

| *Application Runtimes*
| Provides standardized environments for developing and running applications.

| *Application Builds*
| Builds and packages applications into deployable artifacts.

| *Application Delivery (CI/CD)*
| Automates building, testing, deploying, and promoting applications across environments.

| *Observability*
| Provides integrated monitoring, logging, and tracing to understand application health and troubleshoot issues.
|===

These capabilities form the technical foundation of an Application Platform. While organizations can assemble them using independent tools, an Application Platform delivers them as a unified developer experience.

== Beyond Individual Tools

Having the right capabilities is only part of the story.

The real value of an *Application Platform* comes from how those capabilities are integrated and delivered to developers.

Instead of every development team selecting, configuring, and maintaining its own toolchain, the platform provides:

* Standardized development environments
* Automated build and deployment pipelines
* Integrated security and policy enforcement
* Built-in observability
* Self-service templates and Golden Paths

By providing these capabilities through a consistent platform, organizations improve developer productivity, reduce operational complexity, and establish standardized engineering practices across teams.
